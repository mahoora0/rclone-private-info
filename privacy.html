rclone-private Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 18, 2026

Overview

rclone-private is a personal OAuth configuration for managing the owner's Google Drive files through rclone.

Data Access and Use

The configuration may access Google Drive files, folders, and related metadata, as well as account information for connection checks. Data is used only for file management and operations requested by the owner.

Storage and Sharing

File operations are performed through rclone on the owner's device. Authentication credentials are stored locally. This website does not collect or process Google user data.

Google user data is not sold or used for advertising. Data is transferred only as directed by the owner. Use of Google user data complies with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Data Control and Contact

The owner manages data retention and deletion and may revoke access through Google Account settings. Revoking access does not delete existing Google Drive files.

For questions, contact the support email listed on the OAuth consent screen.
